Privacy Policy

ListenPair

Effective date: November 10, 2026

ListenP air ("the App") is an English-Spanish audio matching game. This policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and your choices. The App is built to work entirely on your device — no account is required, and no personal information is collected beyond what is described below .

Information We Collect

The App does not require you to create an account and does not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, or any other personally identifiable information. Any custom categories or word pairs you create inside the App are stored locally on your device only and are never transmitted to us or to any third party. The App does not use any analytics or tracking software of our own.

In-App Purchases

The App offers one optional, one-time in-app purchase ("Unlock Everything") that removes content restrictions. All purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit. We never see, collect, or store your payment card number, billing address, or other payment details — that information is handled directly by Apple under

Apple's own privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

Data Storage and Security

Any data the App keeps is stored only in Apple's standard app storage on your own device. We do not operate our own servers, and we do not have access to your device's data. Because nothing personal is transmitted off your device by the App itself, there is no server-side database of user information for us to secure or that could be breached. Apple may collect anonymized crash diagnostics through its standard platform services as part of normal App Store operation; this is governed by Apple's own privacy policy and is not data we actively collect or control.

Third-Party Services

The App uses Apple's App Store and StoreKit to process the optional in-app purchase, governed by Apple's own privacy policy linked above. The App does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or social media SDKs, and does not share data with any other third party .

Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Because the App collects no personal information from any user , it does not require parental consent under the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPP A) or similar laws. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us using the information below and we will address it.

Your Choices and Rights

Because the App does not collect personal information, there is no personal data for us to provide, correct, port, or erase. Y our custom categories and word pairs remain under your control at all times and can be removed directly within the App, or erased entirely by deleting the App from your device — no request to us is necessary to exercise your right to erasure or data portability . If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCP A/CPRA) gives you rights regarding personal information collected by businesses; if you are in the European Union or European Economic Area, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) gives you similar rights; and the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPP A) protects children under 13. Because the App collects no personal information from any user , these rights are satisfied by design. For users in the EEA: since the App processes no personal data, there is no data controller relationship to establish, but you may still reach us using the contact information below with any questions.
