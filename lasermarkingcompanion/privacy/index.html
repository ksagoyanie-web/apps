Privacy Policy

Laser Marking Companion

Effective date: August 20, 2026

Laser Marking Companion ("the App") is a mobile application for laser marking professionals. It includes educational content, parameter estimation tools, and report generation - all running entirely on your device. This policy is short because our data practices are simple: we collect nothing.

1. Security: We do not collect your data / your data stays on your device

The App does not require an account and collects, stores, or transmits no personal information of any kind. There are no advertisements, no analytics, and no tracking. Nothing you enter ever leaves your device.

All estimations and reports are stored locally on your device. If you export a report via email or file sharing, that action is entirely yours - we never see the content. When you delete the App or clear its data, everything is permanently removed. There is nothing for us to secure or recover on our end.

The App may request storage and share-sheet permissions solely to save your work locally and let you export reports. No permission grants us access to your data. Apple may collect anonymized crash diagnostics through their platforms as part of standard developer services; this is governed by their own policies (linked in Section 2) and is not data we actively collect or control.

2. Payments

All purchases are processed through the Apple App Store. We do not handle your payment or billing information directly. We only receive basic transaction confirmation (such as a purchase unlock signal) to enable your content. For full details on how Apple handle your payment data:

Apple Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

3. Third-party services

The App contains no third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs. System features like email and file sharing are provided by your device's operating system and governed by its own privacy policies.

4. Your privacy rights

Because the App collects no personal data, rights under the GDPR (EU/EEA), CCPA/CPRA (California), and COPPA (children under 13) are satisfied by design. There is nothing to access, correct, port, or erase beyond what already exists on your own device.

To exercise your right to erasure or data portability: delete the App or clear its data in your device settings (erasure), or use the App's export function to transfer your reports (portability). No request to us is necessary.

Children's privacy: This App is intended for professional use and is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where required by applicable law). We collect no data from any user, including minors.

Data controller (EEA users): See Section 6 for contact details.

5. Updates to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted in the App Store listing. Continued use of the App after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
